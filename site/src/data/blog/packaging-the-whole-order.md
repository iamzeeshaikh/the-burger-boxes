The burger box gets the attention, but customers judge the handover as a whole. A branded
box arriving next to a plain sauce pot, a leaking bag and a fries carton from a different
supplier does not read as one brand — it reads as one good decision surrounded by cheaper
ones.

## Fries need the opposite of what the burger needs

A burger wants heat kept in. Fries want steam let out. Put them in the same sealed box and
one of them loses — usually the fries, which go limp in minutes, and often the bun, which
softens from the steam the fries release.

Separate containers solve it, and the fries container should be open or vented rather than
sealed. It is the single highest-impact change most operations can make to how a delivery
order arrives.

## Sauces leak, or they do not travel

Sauce loose in a bag is the most common cause of a ruined order. Pots with lids that
actually seal are cheap and they remove an entire category of complaint. The other option is
to sauce the build and accept that it will not survive a long journey — which is a legitimate
choice, but it should be a decision rather than an accident.

## Drinks crush things

A cup in a bag next to an unsupported box will lean on it. On a long journey that is enough
to deform a single-layer box. If drinks travel with food, either carry them separately or
specify a stiffer board for the boxes underneath — the comparison is under
[burger boxes by material](/product-category/boxes-by-material/).

## The bag is packaging too

A delivery bag is the last thing the customer sees before the food, and it is usually the
least considered item in the order. It is also a large flat surface, which makes it the
cheapest square inch of branding available anywhere in the order.

Bags, cups, sauce pots, fries and nugget boxes, trays, bowls and straws are all listed under
[food packaging for burger shops](/product-category/complementary-food-packaging/).

## Why one run beats four suppliers

Ordering everything on a single run does three things. The artwork matches, because it is
the same files on the same press. The unit cost falls, because it is one setup rather than
four. And there is one lead time to track rather than four that drift apart and leave you
with branded boxes and plain cups for a month.
